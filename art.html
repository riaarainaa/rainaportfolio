ART 3.0

Turning a six-act consulting method into a platform that runs itself.

Year2024 — 2025
RoleInteraction Designer
DurationOngoing
ToolsFigma, FigJam

01 / The Brief

The method worked. The software fought it.

Leadership teams had a rigorous six-act transformation method. By v2.x, consultants re-entered and reformatted the same information at every stage. ART 3.0's premise: the parts that felt like using the tool were the parts that added no thinking.

02 / Process

Ask, don't click. Act, then review.

I mapped every manual touchpoint across the six acts, then ran sessions with consultants to find where judgment was required versus where the tool was in the way. Core principle: automation should make the invisible visible — not replace the expert.

03 / Solution

Every AI change is a visible diff.

A conversational layer replaced form-filling across the platform. AI agents pre-populate transitions between acts based on prior work — but every suggestion appears as a reviewable diff. Consultants approve, edit, or reject. The system never acts without visibility.

04 / Outcome

~85% less setup time between acts.

Directional metrics showed a dramatic reduction in repetitive setup work. More importantly: consultants reported spending more time on judgment. The hardest design question — what should AI not do automatically? — turned out to be the most valuable one.
