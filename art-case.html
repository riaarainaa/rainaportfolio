Beyond task-level efficiency, it gives enterprises a strategic lens on how they operate, compete, and create value, mapping business models and value chains to shift the organisation from reactive optimisation to proactive, continuous reinvention. It democratises knowledge and expertise, breaking down silos to enable an AI-augmented workforce supported by an intelligent digital brain that helps organisations continuously reinvent how work gets done.

Why this existed

ART is built for pursuits where clients need a defensible value case rather than a point of view. That's the heart of it. Consultants could always offer a point of view — "here's what we think you should do." But leadership teams betting millions on a transformation needed proof: what specifically will change, by how much, and can you show me before we commit? ART exists to replace opinion with a quantified, defensible case.

The challenge

For a platform whose entire pitch is accelerate and de-risk reinvention, the workflow itself had become the thing slowing teams down. The goal: cut the manual inputs and fragmented workflows, and shift that time back to strategy and outcome.

ART v1.0

  • Captured inputs, not intent — the why got lost in manual entry.
  • Insight came after the labor — time went to building, not judgment.
  • A rigid, linear path — no shortcut from ambition to model.
What the new flow changed

ART v2.5

The tool built to move clients from insight to impact was spending most of its time upstream of the insight. The 3.0 goal was to close that gap: let AI generate the first-draft model from a stated ambition, so consultants start by reviewing and reasoning — not building.

  • Intent in, model out — one sentence drafts the whole model.
  • Review, don't build — the model arrives ready to adjust and validate.
  • Exploration becomes cheap — models in minutes, so consultants can test directions.

The version we had to rethink

Our first approach let AI decide the single best future state and apply it automatically. Fast and hands-off — but consultants didn't trust one answer applied for them. They couldn't see the reasoning, compare options, or add their judgment. We'd automated away the exact thing they're paid for: deciding how a business should be reinvented.

ART v1.0

So we put the judgment back with the consultant. ART now generates three distinct reinvention paths from the same ambition; the consultant weighs the tradeoffs and picks the best fit, then refines it in conversation. We traded the speed of one automatic answer for a decision they own. AI widens the options, the human makes the call.

Reflection

Intent is where the solution lies

The version that didn't work taught me the most. I assumed the best AI feature was the one that did the most — decide the answer and apply it. Consultants didn't trust it, and they were right: I'd automated away the part of the job they care about.

It showed me how much UX is changing. For years the job was designing screens — arranging what a user sees and clicks, step by step. from laying out screens to shaping how a user and an agent work together: what the agent does on its own, what it brings back, and where the user stays in control.
